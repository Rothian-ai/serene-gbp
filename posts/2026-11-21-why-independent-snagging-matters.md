A snagging report delivered before final payment is leverage. Delivered after, it is only a complaint.

Our guide explains why the developer's own handover team cannot be your inspector: it is checking its own work, scoped to its own liability. The order that protects a buyer runs from independent inspection to a documented defect list, then rectification, and only then release of final payment or mortgage drawdown. For overseas buyers signing off remotely, lining up an inspector before the handover invitation arrives matters most.
