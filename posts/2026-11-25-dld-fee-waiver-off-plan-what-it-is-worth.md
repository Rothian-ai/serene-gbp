A DLD fee waiver saves 4 per cent in cash, but the price on your contract stays exactly where it was.

On a median off-plan apartment the waiver is worth roughly AED 77,645, paid once at registration. A 4 per cent discount saves slightly more in cash and also lowers the recorded price, which a lender, a later buyer and your resale baseline all start from. Our insight sets the two side by side and lists the questions to settle in writing, such as whether the waiver covers the full 4 per cent or only the buyer's share.
