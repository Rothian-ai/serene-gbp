Dubai has nearly 40,000 licensed property brokers, and every one of them is paid only when a deal closes.

Active licensed brokers rose from roughly 5,933 a decade ago to 39,776 by January 2026, all of them on commission alone. Average tenure has fallen to six months or less, so the agent who arranges an off-plan purchase may have left the industry before handover. Our analysis looks at what that pay structure rewards, and the slow work of comparison and verification it does not pay for.
