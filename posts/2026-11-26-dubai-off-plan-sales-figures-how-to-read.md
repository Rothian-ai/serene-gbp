For September 2026, four reports put Dubai's off-plan sales between AED 12.27 billion and AED 15.5 billion. None of them is wrong.

Each report counts something different: homes only or a wider total, off-plan resales inside the off-plan line or not, units or value. A single project can also move a month: of the 1,055 fall in Dubai home sales from August to September, 1,049 came from one development. The insight sets out three checks to make before trusting any monthly figure.
