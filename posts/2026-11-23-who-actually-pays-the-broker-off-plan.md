On an off-plan purchase in Dubai, going direct to the developer does not save you the broker's commission.

On off-plan sales the commission, typically 2 to 8 per cent, is budgeted into the developer's own economics, and the headline price and payment plan are the same with or without a broker. Going direct does not return it as a discount; it removes the one party there to represent you, compare projects and help after handover. Resale works differently: there the buyer usually pays the agent's fee.
