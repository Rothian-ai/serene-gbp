Selling off-plan before handover is an assignment, and the developer's No Objection Certificate expires after 30 days.

Our guide sets out the four moving parts: developer resale approval under your SPA, a Form F memorandum, the developer NOC and an Oqood transfer at a DLD trustee office. It also lists the costs, including the 4 per cent DLD transfer fee and AED 4,000 Oqood administration, which are best added up before you agree terms, particularly when selling from abroad.
