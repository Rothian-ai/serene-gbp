A signed SPA is a contract. The Oqood certificate is what records it, and it is the paper to ask for.

Oqood is the Dubai Land Department register for off-plan sales, filed by the developer between the SPA and the title deed. Under Law No. 13 of 2008, as Khaleej Times summarises it, a sale not entered in the register is void. Our guide sets out what the certificate proves, why registration can lag signing by weeks, and how to check each field against your SPA.
