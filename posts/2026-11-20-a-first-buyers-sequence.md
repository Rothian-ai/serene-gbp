Buying off-plan is a sequence, not an event, and each step carries its own protection.

Our guide lays the purchase out in order, from objective to title deed. Verify the RERA-regulated escrow account before paying the 5 to 10 per cent reservation deposit; read the SPA's late-handover compensation clauses; pay against certified construction milestones, not dates; and have an independent snagging inspection done before the final payment, while you still have leverage.
