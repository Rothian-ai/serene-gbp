When you buy off-plan in Dubai, your money does not go to the developer. It goes to an escrow account regulated by the DLD.

Every payment sits at a DLD-accredited trustee bank in the project's name, and the developer can draw on it only when an independent engineer certifies each construction milestone. If a project stalls, the account is frozen, not lost. The insight sets out the two questions to ask before a deposit: is the account registered and funded, and how is the milestone schedule weighted?
