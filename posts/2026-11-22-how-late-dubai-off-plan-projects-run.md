An off-plan handover date in Dubai is a projection, not a commitment, and the delivery record shows by how much.

Roughly 40 to 50 per cent of Dubai off-plan projects see some delay, averaging around 8.5 months in 2024 to 2026, down from about 12 months in 2018 to 2020. The average hides a wide spread: delays at smaller-tier developers regularly exceed 10 to 18 months. For an overseas buyer, the cost depends on how early a slip is known, and the DLD project tracker makes progress checkable.
