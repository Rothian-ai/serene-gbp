Off-plan was 76 per cent of Dubai sales in Q2 2026, because resales fell 59 per cent, not because launches boomed.

On betterhomes' figures, off-plan sales fell 12 per cent year on year while resales fell 59 per cent, so the share rose as the resale market thinned. That matters for price evidence: with fewer completed homes changing hands, a launch price is harder to check. Our insight sets out what a buyer can compare against instead, from DLD records for the community to resales of a developer's earlier phases.
