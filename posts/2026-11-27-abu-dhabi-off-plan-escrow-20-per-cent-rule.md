In Abu Dhabi, off-plan buyer money stays in escrow until 20 per cent of the build is complete, with one narrow exception.

Under Law No. 2 of 2025, instalments wait in a project escrow account until ADREC-approved engineers verify that 20 per cent mark, and they can never pay for the land or broker commissions. That exception is early release against a bank guarantee, open only to developers with a proven delivery record.
